Entry for the make.fun bounty [One Line, Big Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-coral-404-one-line-big-laugh-328fb857) — one funny line of at most 200 characters, written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The line

> My teacher called me by my brother's name for six years. I graduated as him.

76 characters. A specific observed scene whose last words recontextualise the
setup: six years of being called the wrong name ends with graduating as the
brother. Two beats (the duration, then the identity), a quotable final image,
and a punch in the last three words.

## How it was chosen

402 hand-authored candidates across 10 premise families (household objects,
public rituals, family scenes, small money, bodies and ageing, phones,
food failures, weather, names, absurdly specific detail), screened through
the judge-list-max gate tightened for a top-2-only comp: all 12 checklist
items + at least 3 likes + zero dislikes + at least 2 patterns + the
criterion-5 repeatability test ("would a reader repeat this to a friend
today?"). The keeper passes 12/12 + 5/5 likes + 0 dislikes with P12
echo-close and P2 claim-vs-evidence.

Full pool: `~/workspace/makefun/one-line-big-laugh-01-candidates.py`.
Screening: `~/workspace/makefun/one-line-big-laugh-01-screening.md`.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat output/entry.txt
```

Pure Python 3 stdlib, no dependencies. Deterministic: the same 76 bytes on
every run, no trailing newline.
